Your Notes: This isn't about making huge changes overnight...

What Are Habits?

  • Automatic behaviors we perform without conscious thought
  • The foundation of your results - your outcomes are a reflection of your daily habits
  • One small habit can cascade into massive results

Your Notes: Habits are the invisible architecture...

Results Come From Habits, Not Goals

You want: success, growth, excellence. But real change comes from fixing habits, not chasing results.

❌ Wrong Focus

Chase targets without systems

✅ Right Focus

Build habits that naturally lead to those results

Your Notes: This is the key insight...

The Starting Line Matters Most

  • Getting started is harder than continuing
  • Master the first 5 minutes - everything flows after
  • Challenges don't stop winners - it's about overcoming initial friction

Your Notes: Think about your own experience...

Don't Delay 2-Minute Tasks

  • Small tasks compound into major problems if postponed
  • Wash hands immediately → prevents food safety issues
  • Clean your station → prevents pest problems
  • Count inventory → reveals shortages before next day

Your Notes: This isn't about perfection...

Prime Your Environment

Your environment does half the work for you

✓ Right Setup

Tools and supplies prepped
Everything within reach
Checklists visible
Clear procedures posted

❌ What Breaks It

Searching for what you need
Tools scattered everywhere
No clear workflow
Forgotten procedures

Your Notes: This is about friction...

Make It Fun - The Missing Ingredient

  • People stick with what feels good, not what feels like a hassle
  • Mastering your craft → genuine pride (not just salary)
  • Customer praise heard directly → immediate reward
  • Team wins → celebrate small victories together

Your Notes: The biggest hurdle...

Systems Beat Goals (Always)

❌ Goal

"I want to be successful this year "
(Feels good, no plan)

✓ System

"Execute daily: 2 hours deep work, one conversation, one learning "
(Actual daily process)

Your Notes: Winners and losers...

Never Throw Up A Zero

  • Even a bad day is better than no day
  • Half effort > zero effort because "today was hard "
  • Missed your window? Still show up.
  • Made a mistake? Still execute, learn from it.
  • Momentum builds from consistency, not perfection

Your Notes: This is consistency...

You Don't "Do " Habits - You Become Them

  • Not: "I need to do quality work " | But: "I am someone who does quality work "
  • Not: "I should be on time " | But: "I am reliable "
  • Every action is a vote for the person you're becoming

Your Notes: Identity-based habits...

Stack New Habits on Old Ones

After [existing habit] I will [new habit]

  • After I start my day, I review my priorities
  • After I finish a major task, I take 5 minutes to update my progress
  • After lunch, I do a quick learning activity
  • After my work ends, I plan tomorrow's three key actions

Your Notes: New habits stick...

Reduce Scope, Stick to Schedule

What to do when time is short or you're overwhelmed:

❌ Wrong

"I planned to do X, but I'm behind. Forget it, I'll skip today. "
(You threw up a zero)

✓ Right

"I'll do half of X today, but I'll do it on schedule. "
(You maintained the habit)

Your Notes: Bad days are MORE important...

Track Your Wins Visibly

  • Humans are wired to see progress - make it visible
  • 7 consecutive days completed = small celebration
  • 30 days of consistency = public recognition
  • Visual tracking (checklist, counter, app) keeps habits alive
  • Watching the chain grow is its own reward

Your Notes: Use the paper clip strategy...

1% Better Every Day = 37x Better In a Year

1.01^365 = 37.78 | But also: 0.99^365 = 0.03 (37x worse)

  • You're either improving or declining - stagnation isn't real
  • Small daily improvements compound invisibly... then explosively
  • Month 1: invisible progress. Month 6: noticeable. Year 1: life-changing
  • The hardest part is staying with it during months 1-3
